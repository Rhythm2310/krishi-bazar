# Product Context

**Audience:** Students of United International University (UIU), Bangladesh, typically ranging from 18 to 25 years old. They need quick, accurate calculations for academic standing and financial planning.
**Purpose:** Provide a fast, reliable, fully client-side tool for GPA/CGPA calculation and tuition fee breakdown without requiring logins or server interaction.
**Operating Context:** Web browsers (mobile and desktop). Given the student demographic, a significant portion of usage will likely be on mobile devices on varying network speeds (hence the value of a client-side SPA).
**Constraints:**
- Strict adherence to UIU grading scales and credit systems.
- Must remain fully client-side (no backend).
- Must preserve existing functionality, data models, and business logic exactly.
**Voice:** Helpful, clear, precise, and academic but accessible. No jargon outside of standard university terms (credits, GPA, waivers).

## Genuine Gaps (Questions for the User)
1. **Brand Context:** Should this app strictly follow UIU official branding (e.g., specific orange/blue hues, logos if any were allowed) or should it have a distinct, modern, independent identity (since it's a student tool)? (Default: Modern, independent identity with subtle nods to UIU colors).
2. **Tone/Vibe:** Should the UI feel like an official enterprise university portal, or a modern, sleek, consumer-grade utility app? (Default: Modern utility app—clean, fast, focused).
