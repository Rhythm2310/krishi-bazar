# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
React (SPA), Vite, Plain CSS, Bun

## Users
Students of United International University (UIU), Bangladesh, typically ranging from 18 to 25 years old. They need quick, accurate calculations for academic standing and financial planning.

## Product Purpose
Provide a fast, reliable, fully client-side tool for GPA/CGPA calculation and tuition fee breakdown without requiring logins or server interaction.

## Positioning
A zero-friction, lightning-fast, privacy-first grade and fee calculator built exclusively for UIU's grading systems.

## Operating Context
Web browsers (mobile and desktop). High mobile usage on varying network speeds.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Strict adherence to UIU grading scales and credit systems.
- Fully client-side (no backend).
- Must preserve existing functionality, data models, and business logic exactly.
- Strictly a single page app.

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: Helpful, clear, precise, academic but accessible. No jargon outside of standard university terms.
- Visual Vibe: Modern utility app—approachable, lightweight, ultra-clean, calming color palette. Not the official enterprise portal look. Mobile-first layout. Breathtaking animation and layout density.
